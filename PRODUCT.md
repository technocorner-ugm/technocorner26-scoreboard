# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operator pertandingan Technocorner yang mengatur nama tim, babak, skor, dan timer; peserta serta penonton yang membaca display scoreboard dari perangkat lain di venue.

## Product Purpose

Menyediakan kontrol scoreboard multi-room yang cepat, sinkron, dan mudah dibaca untuk seluruh cabang robotika Technocorner. Keberhasilan berarti aturan tiap cabang tampil tepat, aksi operator tersinkron secara realtime, dan display tidak menimbulkan interpretasi pertandingan yang keliru.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, operational, and precise. The interface should feel recognizably Technocorner while staying calm enough for repeated use during live competition.

## Anti-references

Avoid cyber-themed display fonts, decorative control surfaces, ambiguous match labels, unnecessary realtime status copy, and color-only information without a text or positional cue.

## Design Principles

- Competition rules take precedence over visual consistency.
- Make live operator actions obvious and difficult to misread.
- Keep room, team, timer, and stage context visible at a glance.
- Use Technocorner assets and palette without compromising legibility.
- Preserve stable layouts across operator and display devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support for nonessential animation, and redundant labels for team colors and timer states.
